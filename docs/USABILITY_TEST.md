# Strata volunteer usability session

**Status: not conducted. No participant results are claimed.** Target five to eight volunteers. Do not send invitations automatically. A teammate can arrange sessions with available students/residents; no names or contact details are needed in the results.

## Session

Open the landing page with a fresh session. Say: “Explore San José's satellite heat evidence and prepare a field-review brief. Please say what you think the site is telling you.” Allow up to ten minutes. Do not coach unless a participant is stuck; record assistance.

1. Find San Pedro Square Market and add its surrounding area to comparison.
2. Add the park visitor-center surroundings and explain the difference between the two temperature columns.
3. Explain what −2.2°C means. Successful interpretation: below this image's median surface temperature; it is not below freezing, current weather, or air temperature.
4. Change the review criteria and describe what changed. Successful interpretation: which areas qualify, not their temperatures or a predicted cooling benefit.
5. Add a field-check note and generate a brief or CSV.
6. Name something that needs an on-site check before recommending planting.

Observe whether the participant recognizes the historical date and can distinguish satellite findings from uncollected field observations. Afterwards ask what confused them and whether the brief would help discuss a local place.

## Record honestly

Use one anonymous participant ID per row. Mark each task `independent`, `assisted`, or `not completed`. For the main workflow, count success only when tasks 1, 2, 4 and 5 are completed without coaching. Target at least 80%; report the actual numerator and denominator, including any dropouts. Also report temperature comprehension separately. A small convenience sample establishes preliminary usability, not environmental outcomes or population-wide behavior.

```csv
participant_id,task1,task2,temperature_interpretation,task4,task5,field_check_understanding,main_workflow_independent,minutes,confusion,change_to_make
```

## Follow-through

Fix repeated misunderstandings or broken controls first. Retest the changed task and label it as a retest. Preserve original observations rather than replacing unsuccessful results. Add the actual participant count, completion rate and limitations to the pitch only after sessions have happened.
