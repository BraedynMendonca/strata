Can reflected light recover surface-heat patterns? Thermal readings are evaluation targets, not input features. The observed map is available directly for this date; ML is not required to view it.

Model settings & evaluation design

ExtraTrees uses 220 trees, minimum leaf size 3, six reflectance bands, four spectral indices, and 90/270 m neighborhood context. Eight earlier images train the model; two later images test it. A separate spatial check withholds 600 m blocks.

Sources & methods

Public satellite observations

Model and evaluation

Screen definition

Top quarter of positive model-estimated heat differences, with vegetation index (NDVI) below 0.25. Approximate area calculated on the source grid. The screen does not assess ownership, space for trees, utilities, irrigation, or local priorities.

Interpretation

These are same-day relative heat estimates from reflected-light data on dates excluded from training. The model does not forecast future weather. Thermal targets are centered on each image’s median.

Temperature scores and hotspot precision use a seeded sample of up to 30,000 valid pixels per test date. The headline error reduction compares the average of the two date-specific RMSE values with the average baseline RMSE.

Landsat thermal observations have approximately 100 m native resolution, distributed on a 30 m grid. Display resampling adds no measured detail. Click readouts are rounded to 0.1°C. Two clear-sky dates in one city provide preliminary validation; they do not establish human heat exposure or the causal benefit of a cooling intervention.
